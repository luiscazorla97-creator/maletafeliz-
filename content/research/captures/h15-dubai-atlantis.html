Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about celebrating New Year’s Eve at Atlantis Dubai's Under The Stars Gala Dinner.

About the Gala

What's included in my Gala ticket?

Your Gala ticket includes an iconic New Year’s Eve experience with Katy Perry taking centre stage. Enjoy Champagne and canapés on arrival followed by a lavish Gala dinner buffet featuring lobster, caviar, sushi and live cooking stations. Throughout the evening, you will celebrate with live entertainment and performances before welcoming the New Year with an extraordinary midnight fireworks display.

What's the difference between booking a Golden, Rouge or Premium seat?

The difference between Golden, Rouge and Premium tickets is the location of your seat for the evening. All tickets include Champagne on arrival, Gala dinner buffet and live entertainment. Golden Circle seating benefits from additional inclusions such as caviar and unlimited white truffle service.

View seating arrangements here.

Will there be a New Year’s Eve fireworks celebration?

Yes, experience Dubai's most iconic fireworks show this New Year's Eve at Atlantis Dubai.

Plan your evening

Can I book a Gala ticket with my stay?

Yes, if you’re planning a stay at Atlantis, The Palm or Atlantis The Royal over New Year’s Eve, you must choose between the Gala or one of our restaurant experiences. Your New Year’s Eve experience is booked in addition to your room reservation and can be selected during the booking process.

Are children welcome at the Gala?

Yes, children of all ages are welcome at the Gala.

Is there a dress code for the Gala?

Yes, the dress code is glamorous. Ladies are asked to wear an elegant evening dress or gown and gentlemen are asked to wear a long-sleeved collared shirt, long trousers or smart jeans and closed shoes. UAE national dress is also welcome.
