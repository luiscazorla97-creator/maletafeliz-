 Annual Passport Information ¥5,000 each for one person 
 In an effort to make the museum more accessible and welcoming, Shikoku Mura offers an Annual Passport (Tomo no Kai System) that provides unlimited admission for one year. It offers a variety of benefits to encourage all our Shikoku Mura supporters to visit. We want everyone to come and enjoy Shikoku Mura, no matter the season.
 You can pay the village admission fee or purchase an annual pass using cash, credit cards, electronic money, or various QR/barcode payment services.
