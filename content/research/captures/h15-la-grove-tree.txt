25th Annual Christmas at The Grove
November 16, 2026
Join us for a special Tree Lighting Celebration as we mark a milestone year and kick off the holiday season! The magical evening will be hosted by Lance Bass and feature performances by Nicole Scherzinger, Jason Mraz, Mark Ambor, Jamie Miller, “O” by Cirque du Soleil, American Authors, Kenedi Anderson, JYT and more. Delight in the lighting of the Christmas Tree, the season’s first snowfall, an exciting drone show, a fireworks spectacular and Santa Claus’ grand arrival.
Presented in partnership with Fox11,
The Practice Healthcare and Rivian.
This complimentary event is open to the public and will be filmed for broadcast. By attending, you consent to the use of your image and likeness in the broadcast. Space is limited and parking rates are subject to change.
