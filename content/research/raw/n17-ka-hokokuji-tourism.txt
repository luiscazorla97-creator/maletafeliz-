Hokokuji Temple
Hokokuji Temple
Kanazawa-kaido Area
 Description 
 Basic Information 
 Flower Report 
 Location 
The temple has always been known for the Moso bamboo forest in its precinct, which is why Hokokuji is also called the Bamboo Temple. The principal image is a statue of a seated Shakanyorai. The founder, Tengan Eko, studied in China during the Yuan Dynasty (1271-1368). Tokishu, the autograph collection of his poems, is an important national cultural asset.
Basic Information
 Address 
 2-7-4 Jomyoji, Kamakura city 
 Access 
 From the JR Kamakura Station East Exit bus stop, take the bus bound for Jomyoji and get off at Jomyoji. 3 minutes walk 
 Or, 35 minutes walk from JR Kamakura Station 
 Phone 
 0467-22-0762 
 Fees 
 【Admission fee】
High school students and above: 400 yen
Elementary and junior high school students: 200 yen
*Matcha tea voucher (includes dried sweets): 600 yen
Disabled: Free (disability certificate required)
Seniors living in the city: Free (Fukuju certificate required) 
 Business Hours 
 9:00am-4:00pm
*Matcha tea reception closes at 3:30pm
*Closed from December 29th to January 3rd 
 Note 
 ◆ Experience
【Tea】
Drink matcha tea on a chair in the bamboo garden
Dates: Every day (closed over the New Year holidays)
Time: 9:00am-4:00pm (reception until 3:30pm)
Price: 600 yen for matcha tea with dried sweets (bamboo garden admission fee required)
*No reservation required
*Matcha reception may be temporarily suspended during peak hours 
 【Sunday Zazen Session】
This is a strict Zazen session, so please come prepared.
Date: Every Sunday (open all year round)
Time: 7:30am-9:15am
Registration: 7:00am-7:30am (strict timekeeping)
*In principle, leaving midway is not permitted
*Please wear loose clothing that is easy to move in. In summer, you may sweat, so we recommend bringing a towel and a change of clothes.
*As a rule, this Zazen session will be conducted in Japanese. Interpretation will not be provided during the session.
*The session may be canceled due to weather, etc. (If there are any changes to the session, we will announce them on the official website) 
 ◆Other
・Tripods, monopods, selfie sticks, etc. are prohibited.
・Public toilets available.
