The Cathedral Basilica is an active Catholic Church.
Please be respectful. We are closed for touring during funerals, weddings, and other religious events.
Please check our website calendar for potential closings during normal business hours.
We are closed for touring on Thanksgiving Day November 26th 
All tour guides and docents in the church are volunteers, and we do our best to make you feel welcome.
No tickets are required to visit or tour the Cathedral. We do not sell tickets.
We do not allow scavenger hunts in the Cathedral Basilica.
No professional videography or photo shoots are allowed in the Cathedral Basilica without advance permission.
For our visitor, school, and tour policies, click here .

 schedule changes and closings or scan the QR code below to view it. We have a docent available anytime we are open. The docent can give a tour, answer questions, and direct you to some of the special features of our Cathedral Basilica.
Guided Tours and School Classes
Typically, we have docents available to give tours of the Cathedral Basilica (9 AM – 11:30 AM and 1 PM- 4:30 PM). 
However, there will be no guided tours or classes by anyone before 10 AM each weekday unless you have the tour coordinator’s advance permission. 
If you plan to bring any group (students, classes, adults) into the Cathedral, we require advance notice whether you want us to provide a docent or not. Email – 
cathedralbasilicatour@gmail.com
Tour companies, schools, or individuals can give tours or hold classes in the Cathedral WITH advance permission from the tour coordinator. 
To guarantee a docent is available for a guided tour, 
groups of 10 or more, please email cathedralbasilicatour@gmail.com
Touring hours are 9 AM-11:30 AM and 1 PM – 4:30 PM. Typically, docents are available Monday through Friday during open hours. On weekends, we have limited docent availability.
The Cathedral Basilica is handicap accessible. There is an elevator on Harris Street.
A small donation of $3.00 per adult is appreciated.
To schedule a tour, email: 
cathedralbasilicatour@gmail.com

Masks
You may wear a mask in the Cathedral Basilica if you choose. It is not mandatory.
Pets
Dogs or other animals, reptiles, birds, pigs, etc.  are not allowed in the Cathedral Basilica with the exception of Service Dogs
