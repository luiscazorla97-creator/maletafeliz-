Search Home / Directory / News Releases / Celebrate the Holidays in Downtown San Antonio Celebrate the Holidays in Downtown San Antonio Published on November 18, 2026 SAN ANTONIO (November 18, 2026) — Celebrate the holidays in downtown San Antonio! Enjoy free, family-friendly events, and shop for one-of-a-kind holiday gifts for your loved ones while supporting local small businesses at Market Square and La Villita . Downtown will be very busy the weekend after Thanksgiving, November 27–29. Before heading downtown, attendees are encouraged to check event details, road closures, and parking information at SA.gov/GoDowntown . River Walk Holiday Lights The world-famous San Antonio River Walk is illuminated in thousands of lights from November 27 – January 11 (dawn). Travis Park Events Location: 301 East Travis St. H-E-B Christmas Tree Lighting Ceremony Date: Friday, November 27 Time: 4 - 7 p.m., tree lighting ceremony at 6 p.m. Cost: free Kick off the holiday festivities at the Annual H-E-B Christmas Tree Lighting Ceremony at Travis Park. Festivities include live entertainment, food trucks, letters to Santa, giveaways, holiday crafts, and a special visit from Santa. VIA Metropolitan Transit is offering free rides to the event, and riders can tell their drivers they are heading to the tree lighting. For more information, visit the Facebook event page . Travis Park Christmas Tree Farm Date: November 20 – December 23 Find the perfect Christmas tree at Travis Park’s Christmas tree farm. Travis Park is bringing holiday cheer to downtown San Antonio with a festive Christmas tree farm, presented in partnership with Centro and Holiday Hills Christmas Trees. Visitors can browse and purchase fresh-cut Christmas trees, garlands, and wreaths, perfect for adding seasonal charm to homes and businesses. Delivery is available. For more information and hours, visit the Holiday Hills Christmas Trees website. Holidays on Houston Street Throughout December, residents and visitors can stroll beneath holiday lights, shop local, and enjoy seasonal entertainment with family and friends. Holidays on Houston Street returns to downtown San Antonio with a communal dinner beneath the illuminated tree canopy, festive markets, live music and new culinary events. Holidays on Houston Street Cocktail Crawl , ongoing throughout December; more information A Taste of Holidays on Houston Street , Thursday, December 3, 6:30 p.m.; purchase tickets SATX 5K Presented by Holidays on Houston Street , Friday, December 4, 8 p.m.; register Holidays on Houston Street Market at La Villita , December 4-6 and 18-20; more information Downtown West Chili Classic , Saturday, December 5, noon – 6 p.m.; purchase tickets Holiday Night City at Texas Public Radio , Friday, December 11, 6-10 p.m.; more information Holiday in the Village: Gingerbread Building Competition , December 11-13; more information La Villita Events Location: 418 Villita Street Ford Holiday River Parade Date: Friday, November 27 Time: 6 p.m. Cost: ticketed event The 45th Annual Ford Holiday River Parade is a San Antonio tradition that offers a spectacular one-hour parade along the San Antonio River Walk. This year’s theme, “Holiday Wishes Come True,” features beautifully illuminated floats and the official river lighting ceremony. This ticketed parade begins at 6 p.m. at the Tobin Center, with a live broadcast at 7:05 PM from the Arneson River Theatre. For more information, visit the event website. Holidays on Houston Street Market Experience Date: December 4-6 and 18-20, see event website for hours and more information For the first time, the Holidays on Houston Street Market Experience will take place at La Villita Historic Arts Village, bringing two weekends of holiday shopping, entertainment and seasonal activities to one of downtown San Antonio's most iconic destinations. Visitors can explore a festive marketplace featuring local artisans, specialty vendors and unique holiday gifts while enjoying seasonal programming throughout the village. Set among La Villita's historic plazas and charming pathways, the market will offer a memorable holiday experience in the heart of downtown. For more information, visit the event website. Painting in the Plaza Date: Sunday, December 6 and 20 Time: 11 a.m. - 1 p.m. Cost: free with registration The community is invited to Painting in the Plaza, a free, family-friendly art experience held on the first and third Sundays through May at La Villita Historic Arts Village. Local artist Erica DeHoyos will guide participants in a step-by-step painting class. The program welcomes all skill levels, though space is limited and advanced registration is required. Registration opens one week before each event date. For more information, visit the La Villita website. Chanukah on the River Date: Sunday, December 6 Time: 4-6 p.m. Cost: free In celebration of Chanukah, the Chabad Center for Jewish Life and Learning hosts their annual Chanukah on the River event. This event will feature local entertainers, food, and a menorah lighting at the Arneson River Theatre. For more information, visit the event website. Holiday in the Village: Gingerbread Building Competition Date: December 11-13 La Villita Historic Arts Village will host Holiday in the Village, featuring a Gingerbread Building Competition presented by Chef Johnny Hernandez in collaboration with St. Philip's College. The event showcases gingerbread creations inspired by San Antonio landmarks, designed by St. Philip's College culinary students. It also highlights the college's historic connection to La Villita, where St. Philip's was founded in 1898, celebrating both student talent and downtown San Antonio's heritage. For more information, visit the Holidays on Houston Street website. Craft Thursday Date: Thursday, December 17 Time: 6-8 p.m. Cost: free with registration The community is invited to Craft Thursday, a free, family-friendly DIY series held at La Villita Historic Arts Village. Local pastry artist, Felicia Miranda, will guide participants in a step-by-step cake decorating class. Attendees are encouraged to arrive early to enjoy dinner or a beverage at on-site restaurants. Seating is limited, and advance registration is required; registration opens one week before the event. Downtown Thursday free parking begins at 5 p.m. in City-owned lots and garages. For more information, visit the La Villita website. Bethany Church Annual Christmas Concert Date: Friday, December 18 Time: 6-8 p.m. Cost: free Enjoy a Christmas concert at the Arneson River Theatre performed by Bethany Church Youth Choir. For more information, visit the La Villita website. TubaMeisters Christmas Concert Date: Saturday, December 19 Time: 12 p.m. Cost: free Local favorites, the TubaMeisters, present their annual Christmas concert at the Arneson River Theatre featuring holiday tunes played by tuba, euphonium, and baritone horn players. For more information, visit the TubaMeister’s website. Ricos River Rally Date: Sunday, December 27 Time: 5 p.m. Cost: free The San Antonio River Walk plays host to college football’s most unique pep rally to celebrate the Valero Alamo Bowl. While the head coaches, team captains, and cheerleaders take part in a spirited pep rally at the Arneson River Theatre on the River Walk, the school bands and the rest of the players excite the crowd by making a grand entrance on river cruisers. For more information, visit the event website. Celebrate SA: San Antonio's Official New Year’s Eve Celebration Date: Thursday, December 31 Time: 6 p.m. – midnight Cost: free Ring in the new year at Celebrate SA, the City’s official New Year’s Eve celebration in downtown produced by the San Antonio Parks Foundation and the City. Enjoy live music, carnival rides, food and drinks, and a fireworks show at midnight. For more information, visit the event website. Market Square Events Location: 514 W. Commerce Street Celebración Musical Date: Saturday, November 28 Time: 10 a.m. – 6 p.m. Cost: free Visit Historic Market Square for a special celebration dedicated to musicians and music lovers alike. Enjoy live entertainment, mariachi music, ballet folklorico, working artists, food booths, and holiday shopping specials! The day will feature a special Musician’s Blessing from noon to 1:30 p.m., during which musicians are invited to receive a blessing to honor their craft and creativity. For more information, visit the Market Square website. Photos with Santa Cost: Free Families are invited to enjoy the holidays at Market Square, shop local, and take complimentary photos with Santa Claus, Mrs. Claus, and holiday elves on select December dates. For more information, visit the Market Square website. Holiday Elves Saturday, December 5, 12-2 p.m. Sunday, December 6, 12-2 p.m. Sunday, December 13, 12-2 p.m. Saturday, December 19, 12-3 p.m. Santa & Mrs. Claus Saturday, December 12, 12-2 p.m. Santa Claus Sunday, December 20, 12-2 p.m. Wednesday, December 23, 12-3 p.m. La Gran Tamalada Date: Saturday, December 12 and Sunday, December 13 Time: 10 a.m. – 6 p.m. Cost: free La Gran Tamalada is an annual cultural tradition at Historic Market Square hosted by La Familia Cortez Restaurants. At this family-friendly event, learn the history and traditions of tamales, enjoy storytelling, and listen to live music. For more information, visit the Market Square website. Market Square Weekends: Live Performances, Local Eats & Art in Action Date: Every weekend Time: 10 a.m. – 6 p.m. Cost: free Enjoy music, working artists, and food booths at Market Square every weekend. For more information and the entertainment lineup, visit the Market Square website. Downtown Thursday Date: Every Thursday after 5 p.m. through the end of 2026 Discover something new every Thursday night with Downtown Thursday, a weekly lineup of arts, culture, music, food, and family‑friendly experiences presented by the City of San Antonio through the end of 2026. Visitors can explore specials offered by more than 30 local businesses, including restaurants, bars, coffee shops, retailers, tour operators, and arts venues. Free parking is available after 5 p.m. in City‑owned facilities, making it easy for guests to enjoy a Thursday night downtown. An online parking map lists all facilities offering free Thursday parking, and the Downtown Thursday website includes details on partner events and special offers. Visitor Parking For easy, convenient, and affordable parking, visit the City’s SAPark website . The parking map provides a full list of City-owned garages and lots. Event parking rates may apply at some locations during busy weekends. The St. Mary’s Garage (205 East Travis St.) and the City Tower Garage (60 North Flores St.) are located just steps from the River Walk and Houston Street and generally, there are many parking spots. Downtown Thursday offers free parking on Thursday from 5 p.m. to 2 a.m. in City-owned parking facilities, and City Tower Sundays offers free parking on Sundays from 7 a.m. to midnight at the City Tower Garage.
