Accessibility at Enchanted
Terrain along the Carved show route varies and may include loose dirt, mulch, grass, and hard-packed dirt paths. The route is illuminated for the evening experience, but lighting levels may be dim in certain areas, which may affect visibility. The Carved route can be navigated with wheelchairs, walkers, and other mobility aids.
What's new at Enchanted?
All your favorites will return with a few exciting changes and additions. Enchanted Forest of Light will debut a laser light show on
the Main Lawn ending in a “snowfall” finale, Tom Fruin’s luminous house sculptures move to the Oak Grove, and Giant Koi will appear in the Garden of Good Fortune to celebrate the Japanese Gardens’ 60th anniversary. A Holiday Market featuring local craftspeople will also take place December 2–6 on the Main Lawn.
