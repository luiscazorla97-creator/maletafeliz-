Guidelines for Visitors Oura church, as a place of prayer, is a cherished heritage preserved by believers. To maintain a prayerful atmosphere and ensure a peaceful time for all visitors, please adhere to the following guidelines. 〇 Taking photographs inside the church is prohibited, but exterior shots are allowed. Please refrain from taking photographs inside the church premises before paying the admission fee. 〇 Eating, drinking, and smoking are not allowed on the church premises. Please refrain from visiting while intoxicated. 〇 We kindly ask that you maintain a quiet atmosphere inside the church and refrain from using mobile phones. 〇 Please do not enter areas marked with "No Entry" signs. 〇 Please do not touch or move any items inside the church, and refrain from taking anything except pamphlets. 〇 Pets (except for guide dogs) are not allowed on the premises. About Universal design There is a long staircase from the square in front of the cathedral to the entrance of the church. There are handrails at both ends of the stairs, but there is no other way to enter the cathedral other than stairs, and wheelchair users need assistance to move up the long stairs. Access Map Access to the Oura Church Access from JR Nagasaki station. About Parking Lot There is no parking available for visitors.
