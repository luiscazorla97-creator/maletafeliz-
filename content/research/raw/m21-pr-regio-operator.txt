Guided 
tours guided tours
You can discover the Teatro Regio and its history by taking a guided tour of the Foyer, the Main Auditorium and the Ridotto, available in Italian and English and lasting approximately 30 minutes. Groups are advised to book in advance. The tour route is fully accessible.
When rehearsals or performances are taking place, some areas may not be accessible and guided tours may be suspended; we therefore invite you to contact us to check availability.

Bookshop
At the bookshop, located at the theatre entrance and run by the Azzali family – the city’s long-established publishers – you can buy a unique item as a gift for someone else or for yourself.
You’ll find the Regio’s new merchandise collections, all the theatre’s publications, librettos, CDs, DVDs, music-related items, posters and playbills for operas and shows, postcards, magnets and a wide range of souvenirs, so you can take home a unique memento of the theatre and Parma.
The bookshop will maintain the same opening and closing days and the same times for guided tours.
SUGGESTED ITINERARIES
Recommended places and monuments to help you continue exploring the history of the city of Parma and the lives of the figures who have contributed to Parma’s great musical tradition:
Museo Glauco Lombardi
For a charming journey in the footsteps of Duchess Marie Louise. 
www.museolombardi.it
Casa natale Arturo Toscanini
To learn more about the life and career of the renowned maestro Arturo Toscanini. 
www.museotoscanini.it
La Casa della Musica
Housed within the Renaissance Palazzo Cusani, La Casa della Musica is home to the Museo dell’Opera and other spaces waiting to be explored.
www.lacasadellamusica.it
