 Description Savannah - Bonaventure Cemetery - Overview.jpg 
 English: 
 A panorama of Bonaventure Cemetery near Savannah, Georgia. A sandy road winds towards the ocean and is flanked by graves with low tombstones, numerous trees, and flowering bushes.
