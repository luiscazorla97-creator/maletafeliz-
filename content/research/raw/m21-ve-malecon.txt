 Macroplaza del Malecón
 The great window to the sea of Veracruz and the social heartbeat of the entire port. This wide pedestrian esplanade invites locals and tourists to enjoy the coming and going of imposing commercial vessels and Navy ships, surrounded by the Gulf breeze. It is the perfect setting to savor a traditional ice cream, enjoy spontaneous live music, watch folkloric shows, and witness beautiful ocean sunsets.
