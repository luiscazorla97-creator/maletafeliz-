What is NYE?
NYE is an annual celebration in Downtown Dubai featuring a fireworks show, laser and light shows, fountain show, and Burj Khalifa show.
What time do the New Year’s Eve celebrations start?
Celebrations in Downtown Dubai start from 5 PM onwards.
Who can attend the celebrations?
We offer a variety of options to enjoy the New Year’s Eve celebration: Paid Experience, Free Viewing Areas.
Where can I park at Dubai Mall?
You can park your car in any of the public parking across Sheikh Mohammed Bin Rashid Boulevard, Zabeel Parking, and Fountain Views Parking on a first-come, first-served basis.
What time can I arrive at Dubai Mall?
Authorities typically close Lower Financial Centre Road around 3 PM, but this can be adjusted based on crowd conditions.
What time does the car park close?
The parking areas themselves will not be closed, but access might be restricted due to road closures.
What time do the roads close?
Road closures are expected to start as early as 4 PM, depending on crowd conditions.
What time can I leave Dubai Mall parking?
Parking is available for exit between 1-2 AM, depending on authorities’ directives.
How do I get a badge for access to Dubai Mall restaurants?
After confirming your reservation, the restaurant will provide instructions on how to obtain your badge.
Will the metro station to Dubai Mall be operational?
Yes, the metro will be operational but may close depending on crowd conditions. Check the RTA website for updates.
What entertainment is available at Dubai Mall?
Dining experiences with live music, large LED screens showcasing the fireworks, and exclusive NYE offers.
What facilities are available at Dubai Mall?
Multiple restrooms, lost and found areas near the promenade, first aid tents, and prayer rooms are available.
What food options are available at Dubai Mall?
Dining options will be available inside Dubai Mall with exclusive NYE menus and offers.
Where can I park at Dubai Hills Malls?
Parking is available at Dubai Hills Mall, with first-come, first-served access.
How do I access the Dubai Hills Mall for NYE events?
Access will be managed through designated entry points; early arrival is advised.
Where can I park for Burj Park?
Before 4 PM
: You can park in public parking areas along Sheikh Mohamed Bin Rashid Boulevard, Zabeel Parking, or Fountain Views Parking (first come, first served).
After 4 PM
: Parking will be available at Al Wasl Club Parking and Al Meydan – Falcon Heritage and Sports Centre. Shuttle buses will transfer you to the venue. You must present your badge to use the shuttle service and get off at Dubai Opera bus stop.
Who can attend the Burj Park New Year’s Eve celebrations?
Burj Park is a paid experience open to all age groups. Tickets are required for entry. Visit Burj Park section for more details.
What time do the celebrations at Burj Park start?
Gate open from 12 noon with entertainment beginning at 5 PM.
What celebrations are planned for Burj Park?
Burj Park will feature uninterrupted views of the Burj Khalifa show, laser and light shows, fireworks displays, F&B food trucks and stalls, live entertainment, and more.
How do I get a badge for Burj Park?
Badges are allocated to guests who purchase tickets through Platinum List and are required for entry.
Dates
: You can pick up your badge between December 26th and 30th, 2025, from Dubai Mall, or Dubai Hills Mall. Weekdays: 10AM to 11PM; Weekends (Friday, Saturday & Sunday): 10AM to 12AM.
Important
: Badge collection is mandatory for entry to Burj Park and specific areas. There will be no badge collection or validation on December 31st. Make sure to collect your badge by December 30th, no later than 10 PM.
Please Note
: Tickets with collected badges won’t be reissued, and lost badges cannot be replaced. Keep your tickets and badges safe.
What is the purpose of a wristband at Burj Park?
Wristbands will be issued after your badge validation and allow access to event facilities on December 31st.
What entertainment is planned for NYE26 at Burj Park?
DJ performances, live bands, and kids’ workshops are part of the entertainment plan.
When should I arrive at Burj Park for New Year's Eve celebrations?
Guests can enter Burj Park from 12 noon, and road closures are expected as early as 4 PM.
What facilities are available at Burj Park?
We’ve got everything you need for a comfortable evening:
• Restrooms: Separate facilities for men and women.
• Seating: Enjoy picnic tables, drum tables, and bean bag seating (first come, first served).
• Kid-Friendly: Special workshops and areas designed for children.
Is there a lost and found area in Burj Park?
Yes, there will be a lost and found service near the main entrance.
What items are restricted in Burj Park?
The following items are not allowed inside the venue:
Weapons of any kind
Illegal substances
Alcohol
Glass containers
Fireworks or other explosives
Pets
Professional photography and recording equipment (e.g., tripods, drones) without prior authorization.
Laser Pointers, permanent markers / felt type pens, scissors or aerosol canisters
Horns, trumpets, sirens, whistles, air horns or Vuvuzela type of noise making devices
Flags, banners, posters or signs larger than 30cm x 30cm, flagpole not attached, and content must not be offensive in any nature
Outside food and beverages (except for necessary baby items)
What events are planned around Burj Khalifa?
Burj Khalifa will come alive with dazzling fireworks, immersive laser and light shows, spectacular live performances, and other multi-sensory experiences to ring in the New Year.
What are the best places to view the Burj Khalifa show?
Public viewing areas include spaces along Sheikh Mohammed Bin Rashid Boulevard. Burj Park will once again be the ideal location for guests looking to enjoy the iconic Burj Khalifa light, music, and fireworks show in an exclusive, ticketed setting.
How do I get access to hotels and restaurants during NYE?
Guests with reservations will receive badges for access to their designated dining areas.
How do I get a badge for hotel and restaurant reservations?
Badges will be provided by hotels or restaurants after confirming reservations.
Do badges provide priority access?
Badges do not grant priority vehicle access after road closures.
What celebrations are planned for Hotels and Restaurants in Downtown Dubai?
Premium experiences at hotels/restaurants will include views of the Burj Khalifa Show, gourmet meals, and entertainment.
When should I arrive at my hotel or restaurant?
Arrive ahead of road closures, which may begin as early as 4 PM.
*Please keep checking the Road and Transport Authority (RTA) website for the most recent information on road closures, public transport status, and timings.
Can hotel guests access the Burj Khalifa viewing area?
For hotel staying guests, badge access is only valid for accessing your respective hotel and does not apply to other paid or reserved venues.
What items are restricted in Hotels and Restaurants?
Fireworks, confetti poppers, alcohol brought from outside, and pets are restricted.
What entertainment options are available at hotels and restaurants on New Year’s Eve?
Entertainment is subject to the respective outlet’s arrangement. Please inquire prior to booking confirmation.
How can EMAAR residents access their buildings during NYE?
Residents must register through the Emaar One App to obtain access badges for NYE.
Are there any restrictions for guests visiting EMAAR Residences during NYE?
EMAAR residents must register their guests on the Emaar One App prior to the given deadline and provide them with badges for access. There will be further communication to EMAAR residents.
How do I get a badge for EMAAR Residences during NYE?
Register yourself and your guests via Emaar One App to collect your badges.
What is the purpose of a badge?
Badges allow authorities to identify residents for smooth access to buildings.
Where can I park if I live in EMAAR Residences?
Before 4 PM:
No vehicles will be allowed access once the road closures begin, so please ensure you arrive before then. Your guests can park in public areas along Sheikh Mohamed Bin Rashid Boulevard, Zabeel Parking, or Fountain Views Parking, on a first-come, first-served basis.
After 4 PM:
Parking will be available at Al Wasl Club and Al Meydan – Falcon Heritage and Sports Centre. Shuttle buses will transport you or your guests to the Downtown area. Please present your badge to use the shuttle service.
Can guests park at EMAAR Residences during NYE?
Guest parking is limited. Inform guests to use public parking or drop-off points.
Before 4 PM:
No vehicles will be allowed access once the road closures begin, so please ensure you arrive before then. Your guests can park in public areas along Sheikh Mohamed Bin Rashid Boulevard, Zabeel Parking, or Fountain Views Parking, on a first-come, first-served basis.
After 4 PM:
Parking will be available at Al Wasl Club and Al Meydan – Falcon Heritage and Sports Centre. Shuttle buses will transport you or your guests to the Downtown area. Please present your badge to use the shuttle service.
Are there dedicated viewing areas for EMAAR residents?
As an EMAAR resident in Downtown Dubai, you’ll enjoy being within walking distance of various public viewing areas. All residents and their invited guests must register on the Emaar One App prior to the deadline in order to obtain badges. Badges are only for seamless entry to your residential building; however, this does not apply to access any paid or reserved venue.
What time can I arrive?
Arrive by 2 PM to avoid delays from road closures starting at 4 PM.
What time do the roads close?
Roads are expected to close as early as 4 PM.
*Please keep checking the Road and Transport Authority (RTA) website for the most recent information on road closures, public transport status, and timings.
Where and when can I collect my badge?
Badges can be collected from your building’s reception between December 26th – 30th.
Can someone else collect the badge for me?
No, only owners/tenants can collect their respective badges.
