How to Get to Mt. Inasa, Nagasaki: By Car, Ropeway & Slope Car — One of the World's Top 3 Night Views

Mt. Inasa in Nagasaki is one of the iconinc landmarks, offering breathtaking panoramic night view of the city.

This guide introduces different ways to access Mt. Inasa, one of the world’s top three night views, by ropeway, slopecar and car. Pick the most convenient way that fits your needs and get ready to experience the beauty of Mt. Inasa.
 

The Nagasaki Inasayama Slope Car is currently out of service due to maintenance work being carried out to ensure safe operation.
Information regarding the resumption of service will be announced once it has been decided.

Overview

  • Breathtaking Night View of Nagasaki City
  • Beautiful Panoramic Views by Day and Night
  • 3 Ways to Reach Mt. Inasa Observatory from JR Nagasaki station
  • Mt. Inasa Observatory “Night View Tour”
  • Must-see Spots around Nagasaki City

Breathtaking Night View of Nagasaki City

Nagasaki is renowned for its stunning night views. The city’s illuminated skyline, bustling harbor and surrounding hills come together to create a captivating scene that is widely admired. In 2021, the night view of Nagasaki city is internationally recognized as one of the “World’s New Top 3 Night Views,” alongside Monaco and Shanghai. Furthermore, at the Night View Summit 2024 in Kita-kyushu, Nagasaki was also selected as one of the “Japan’s New Top 3 Night Views” for the fourth consecutive time along with Kitakyushu and Yokohama. Don’t miss the chance to enjoy Nagasaki’s spectacular night scenery.

Beautiful Panoramic Views by Day and Night

  • Mt. Inasa at night

    In addition to the famous “10 Million Dollar Night View,” visitors can also enjoy unique nighttime scenery enhanced by creative lighting effects.
    At the world's first "Night View Lantern", a one-of-a-kind attraction that should be experienced at least once in a lifetime, constellations and heart-shaped patterns appear to float within the city lights below.Enjoy these captivating night views, which change throughout the year with the seasons, special occasions, and events.
    View more

  • Mt. Inasa during the daytime

    From the summit, which stands 333 meters above sea level—the same height as Tokyo Tower—visitors can enjoy a breathtaking 360-degree panoramic view overlooking the city of Nagasaki. On clear days, the view extends as far as Unzen, Amakusa Islands, and the Goto Islands.Be sure not to miss the magical “golden-to-blue hour” at dusk, when the scenery transforms from sunset into night, creating a truly enchanting atmosphere.

Mt. Nabekanmuri Observatory - A lesser-known but locally recommended spot

Mt. Nabekanmuri stands at 169 meters tall, offering a panoramic view of Nagasaki City. In particular, the Nagasaki Port area and the International Cruise Ship Terminal can be seen from the spacious observation deck. On a clear sunny day, visitors will also be able to spot 5 components of the “Sites of Japan’s Meiji Industrial Revolution: Iron & Steel, Shipbuilding and Coal Mining”, which is registered as UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

Details

3 Ways to Reach Mt. Inasa Observatory from JR Nagasaki station

Mt. Inasa Observatory can be accessed in several ways. Below are the available transportation options from Nagasaki Station. Compare and choose the one that suits your itinerary and preferences. Estimated travel times are based on route information from Google Maps.

 

By Bus & Ropeway
 
  • Fare (Adult)
    • Bus: 190 JPY
    • Ropeway
      • Round trip: 1,900 JPY
      • One way: 1,040 JPY
  • Estimated Time
    • About 20–30 minutes from Nagasaki Station
By Bus & Slope Car
 
  • Fare (Adult)
    • Bus: 220 JPY
    • Slope Car
      • Round trip: 1,000 JPY
      • One way: 600 JPY
  • Estimated Time
    • About 30–40 minutes from Nagasaki Station
By Car
 
  • Parking Fees
    • Summit Observatory Parking Lot: 1,000 JPY per 24 hours
    • Inasayama Midway Parking Lot: Free
    • Nagasaki Ropeway Fuchi-jinja Station Parking Lot: Free
  • Estimated Time
    • About minimum 15 minutes from Nagasaki Station
    • Travel time varies depending on where you park your car.
  • Notes
    • The Summit Observatory Parking Lot is unavailable between 18:00 and 22:00 on Saturdays, Sundays, public holidays, and designated restriction days.
    • If you park at the Inasayama Midway Parking Lot, you must take the slope car to the summit.
    • If you park at the Nagasaki Ropeway Fuchi-jinja Station Parking Lot, you must take the ropeway to the summit.

As of April 2026

 
Bus ＆ Ropeway　　　

 

  • ① Nagasaki Station Bus Stop

    Take the Nagasaki Bus route No.3 or No.4 line bound for Shimo-ohashi(下大橋), Koebaru(小江原) or Aigawa(相川) from Nagasaki Station(長崎駅前) bus stop. 

  • ② Ropeway-mae Bus Stop

    Get off at Ropeway-mae (ロープウェイ前) bus stop and walk to Ropeway Fuchi-jinja Station. (Approx. 3mins)

  • ③ Nagasaki Ropeway Fuchi-jinja Station

    Buy the ticket at counter. Inside the station, you can get various souvenirs including the Nagasaki Ropeway original items. 

  • ④ Nagasaki Ropeway Inasa-dake Station

    Enjoy 5-minutes-ride of Nagasaki ropeway and get off at Nagasaki Ropeway Inasa-dake Station. Then, walk to the observatory for 3mins.

  • Nagasaki Bus Official Website
  • Nagasaki Ropeway Official Website

Nagasaki Ropeway Free Shuttle Bus

Nagasaki Ropeway free shuttle bus service connecting the city to Ropeway Fuchi-jinja Station is available only between 19:00 to 22:00 everyday. *Advance reservation required

Reservation
 
Bus ＆ Slope Car　　

 

  • ① Nagasaki Station Bus Stop

    Take the Nagasaki Bus route No.5 line bound for Inasayama-koen(稲佐山公園) from Nagasaki Station (長崎駅前) bus stop. (Approx. 14mins)

  • ② Mt. Inasa Bus Stop

    Get off at Inasayama-koen(稲佐山公園) bus stop. Then, walk to Mt. Inasa Chufuku Station(稲佐山中腹駅). (Approx. 3mins)

  • ③ Inasayama Slope Car Chufuku Station

    Buy a ticket for slope car at the station and get on the slope car.

  • ④ Inasayama Slope Car Sancho Station

    Enjoy 7-minutes-ride of Inasayama slope car and get off at Inasayama Slope Car Sancho Station. Then, walk to the observatory for 3mins.

  • Nagasaki Bus Official Website
  • Inasayama Slope Car Official Website
 
Car　　


If you visit by car, you can use one of the following parking areas: the Summit Observatory Parking Lot, the Midway Parking Lot, or the Fuchi-jinja Station Parking Lot. Please note that if you park at the Midway Parking Lot or the Fuchi-jinja Station Parking Lot, an additional fee is required for the slope car or ropeway to reach the observatory.


 
Summit Observatory Parking Lot
 
  • Capacity
    • 38 vehicles
    • Parking fee required
  • Opening Hours
    • Open 24 hours, every day
    • Not available from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM on Saturdays, Sundays, public holidays, and during designated peak seasons.
  • Access to the Observatory
    • Approximately 2 minutes on foot from the parking lot
Midway Parking Lot
 
  • Capacity
    • 400 vehicles
    • Free parking
  • Opening Hours
    • 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM, every day
  • Access to the Observatory
    • After parking, take the slope car from Inasayama Slope Car Chufuku Station
Fuchi-jinja Station Parking Lot
 
  • Capacity
    • 14 vehicles
    • Free parking
  • Opening Hours
    • 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM, every day
  • Access to the Observatory
    • After parking, take the Inasayama Ropeway from Fuchi-jinja Station

Must-see Spots around Nagasaki City

Before heading up to Mt. Inasa for the night view, take some time to explore nearby attractions in Nagasaki City. Here are some must-visit spots around Mt. Inasa and nearby areas!

  • Nagasaki Atomic Bomb Museum

    On August 9, 1945, at 11:02 a.m. - three days after the first atomic bomb was dropped on Hiroshima - the second atomic bomb was dropped over the Urakami district of Nagasaki. This bomb killed or injured 150,000 people. Nagasaki Atomic Bomb Museum covers the history of this event in the accessible form of a story. 

    View more

  • Peace Park

    Nagasaki Peace Park is built on a low hill to the north of the hypocenter of the atomic bomb blast. It was created to represent the wish for world peace and a vow that such a tragic war would never be repeated. 

    View more

  • Nagasaki Stadium City

    Nagasaki Stadium City, which have opened on October 14 of 2024, is a new must-visit attraction in Nagasaki prefecture.
    This facility contains a home stadium for “V-Varen Nagasaki” which is a professional soccer club in Japan, and a home arena for “Nagasaki Velca”, one of the professional basketball teams in Japan.

    View more
