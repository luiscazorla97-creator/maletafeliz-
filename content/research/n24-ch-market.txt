Welcome! The Charleston City Market is one of America’s oldest public markets and South Carolina’s number one most visited attraction. For generations, the Charleston City Market has been a place where local artisans and craftspeople, residents, and visitors come together. Today, our iconic Great Hall and historic open-air sheds remain one of Charleston’s most recognizable and beloved shopping destinations. Located in the heart of historic downtown Charleston, the Market stretches across four city blocks and is home to more than 300 vendors offering an extraordinary variety of locally made art, crafts, food, gifts, and souvenirs. We are especially proud of our authentic local sweetgrass basket weavers , whose distinctive handmade baskets represent an important and enduring tradition of Charleston’s Gullah culture. Open every day except December 25 , the Charleston City Market welcomes millions of visitors each year. Celebrate with Us!
