Bivacco
A bold, Italian restaurant serving simple, unfussy fare built from locally sourced ingredients. Bivacco is a destination for slow lunches, memorable dinners, after work drinks, and weekend feasts, with stunning views of the harbour.

Esther
Esther brings a taste of the Mediterranean to the convivial ground floor of Auckland's QT hotel. Under the guidance of executive chef Sean Connolly, Esther mixes simplicity and wonder from their inviting country-style kitchen.
