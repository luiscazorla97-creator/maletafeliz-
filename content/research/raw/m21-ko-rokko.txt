 Rokko Garden Terrace 
 Rokko Garden Terrace 
Loading...
The exotic Rokko Garden Terrace area features four restaurants and two shops, a leisure spot for the best seasonal treats and shopping. The area is peppered with lookouts offering stunning panoramic views. Akashi Strait, Osaka Plains and Kansai International Airport are all within sight and at night, you can soak up the “Ten million dollar night view.”
