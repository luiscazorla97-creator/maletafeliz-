The Castle does not have its own parking facilities, but paid parking is available nearby at: Buonconsiglio multi-storey car park; Piazza Fiera underground car park; Centro Europa underground car park, two levels down. HOW TO GET TO TRENTO By car The Brenner motorway (A22, exit Trento Nord or Trento Sud), state road 12 of Abetone and Brenner (SS12) from Verona or Bolzano, state road 47 of Valsugana (SS47 Valsugana Superstrada) from Venice, and state road 45bis Gardesana Occidentale (SS45bis) for those coming from Brescia and Lake Garda. By train The Brenner line and the Valsugana line from Venice. The train station is located near the historic centre of Trento (Piazza Duomo is just a short walk away). At the station, you can also find the terminus for the Trento-Malè-Mezzana local train line coming from Val di Sole and the branch of the Valsugana line which leads to Bassano del Grappa and Venice. By plane The nearest airports are: Verona (90 km.), Brescia (165 km.), Venice (155 km.), Treviso (130 km.), Milano (250 km.), Bergamo (200 km.) and Bologna (225 km.). By bus Trento is well connected to all towns in the province by an efficient bus service, making it easy to reach any town in Trentino; timetables are available on the Trentino Trasporti page . Furthermore, there is a direct multi-week connection between Trento and some European cities (Prague, Vienna, Innsbruck, Salzburg, Ceske Budejovice), while, in some periods of the year, the same service is carried out towards some Italian cities. Finally, there is the Flixbus service which can be consulted on the page dedicated webpage. DOWNLOAD THE MAP APP There is an APP dedicated to visit the Castello del Buonconsiglio . The APP is free for Apple / Android devices and it offers four itineraries: a standard tour, one with video descriptions in Italian Sign Language (LIS) with subtitles, one with more detailed audio descriptions for blind or visually impaired visitors, and a family-friendly route featuring animations. To use it, an internet connection is required and it is recommended to enable Bluetooth to enhance the visitor experience. No account is needed. To download the app, access the App Store on your device, search for the app name in the search bar or use the dedicated link , and select “Install.” For further details, please visit the FAQ page . DOWNLOAD THE APP PHYSICAL ACCESSIBILITY Visiting Buonconsiglio Castle in Trento and all its other sites may pose challenges for anyone with mobility difficulties. BUONCONSIGLIO Buonconsiglio Castle has stairs and other physical barriers and there are no elevators. The garden, library and cafeteria can be accessed by a convenient ramp at the Porta San Martino even in a wheelchair. You can admire the frescoes in the Clesian Refectory via the elevator inside the cafeteria. In any case, it is recommended you contact the Museum (tel. +39 0461 492811 and dedicates email address ) for more precise information regarding access arrangements.
