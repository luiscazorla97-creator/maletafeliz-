Paper passes can be purchased at the Sasebo Bus Center and at the Sasebo Tourist Information Center inside JR Sasebo Station. Nishi Kyushu Shinkansen JR Kyushu Rail Pass This pass is ideal for travelling throughout Kyushu as there are pass types available that offer access to Northern Kyushu (Fukuoka, Saga, Nagasaki, Oita and Kumamoto), Southern Kyushu (Oita, Kumamoto, Miyazaki and Kagoshima), or Kyushu in its entirety. This pass also gives users the ability to freely select seats on local trains, limited express trains, and Shinkansen (bullet trains). Notice; When you get on the train, be sure to show your passport to prove that you are an international visitor. The passport must have visa stamp or seal for a temporary visitor. JR Kyushu Rail Pass JR Kyushu Rail Pass Online Booking SUNQ PASS SUNQ PASS is a free pass ticket that allows unlimited rides on almost all Expressway Bus and local buses in the seven prefectures in Kyushu and the Shimonoseki, Nagato area (Yamaguchi), as well as on some ferries. There are 5types of the ticket depending on the area and validity period.
