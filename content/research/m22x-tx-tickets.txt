Program Details Step into a glowing wonderland filled with all-new handcrafted lanterns, dazzling light displays, and larger-than-life illuminated sculptures. Wander through radiant trails that wind through the beauty of the Garden, creating unforgettable moments for families, date nights, and holiday outings with friends. General Admission Illuminate Tickets (Mon – Sun) Public Ticket Pricing: $29.99 for adults and $26.99 for children (children under 3 are free), plus applicable fees. Member Ticket Pricing*: $24.99 for both adults and children (children under 3 are free), with no added fees. Tickets are sold in 30-minute time slots, which serve as your designated entry time to the exhibition. A limited number of walk-up tickets may be available at the front kiosk, depending on remaining inventory. Ticket prices do not include parking. All discounts are applied automatically at checkout. Please note that when member fees are waived, the discount is deducted from the ticket price , but the fee line item will still appear in the order summary. General admission ticket sales end 3 hours before each time slot ; afterward, only Same-Day tickets will be available online for an additional $5 per ticket. General admission sales close 3 hours before each time slot—after that, only Same-Day tickets are available online with a $5/ticket premium (available day-of only, not in advance). Flex Tickets Experience the enchantment of Illuminate at the San Antonio Botanical Garden on any day you choose, with no need to commit to a specific date. Great for gift-giving! 2026 Illuminate Flex Any Day Single Ticket: Available at a flat rate of $39.99 for both adults and children. Please note that member discounts do not apply to flex tickets. Tickets are non-refundable. A $10 rescheduling fee applies to all date or time changes. The Garden is closed for Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day. For December 1st only, ticket holders who pick timed entry between 5:30 – 7 p.m. have the choice to enjoy the trail without sound or can bring their own headphones for an individual audio experience.
