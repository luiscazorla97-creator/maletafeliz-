Stellenbosch Museum | Stellenbosch Museum THE SCHREUDERHUIS The first house the museum has restored is the Schreuderhuis. This home was built in 1709, and it survived the first great fire in Stellenbosch. Schreuderhuis is the oldest restored and documented town house...
