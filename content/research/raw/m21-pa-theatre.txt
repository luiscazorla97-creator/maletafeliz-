 Standard Guided Tour
Get to know the Massimo with a guided tour: explore the stunning auditorium and the most interesting halls of the third largest opera house in Europe.
The tour lasts about 40 minutes and is available in Italian, English, French, Spanish and German. 
Groups are welcome to reserve the tour in advance and may request to visit the theatre during closing time as well (in this case booking is compulsory).
The tour is accessible.
Explore the Teatro Massimo with our audio and video guides
. The guided tour of the theatre has a reduced cost of 6 euros per person for people with disabilities and one accompanying person. 3D models and tactile maps are available to enhance the experience of the blind and partially blind.

 Backstage Tour
Visit the stage and see what’s behind an opera, a concert or a ballet at the Massimo! 
You will never experience the same tour twice, as the stage changes according to what is being performed. 
Booking is compulsory for this tour and must be planned according to production times (the stage will not be accessible during rehearsals, performances and technical work).
The backstage is partially accessible to people with reduced mobility or on a wheelchair.
Tickets
 € 5 (plus the standard guided tour ticket)
 Cocktail Tour
You can book a special guided tour that includes a cocktail in the sitting room of the Royal Box.
The tour is offered to groups of at least 15 and up to 30 people. Booking is compulsory (at least 7 days in advance). 
This tour is accessible and lasts about 60 minutes.  
Tickets
 € 55,00
No concessions available; the price may vary if you request a tour out of the Theatre’s opening hours.
 Concert & Cocktail Tour
This option, 
available to groups ranging from 30 to 100 people
, includes a special concert in one of the Halls of the Theatre, a cocktail in the sitting room of the Royal Box and the guided tour of the opera house. 
Booking is compulsory (at least 7 days in advance). 
This tour is fully accessible and lasts about 90 minutes.
Tickets
 € 95,00
No concessions available; the price may vary if you request a tour out of the Theatre’s opening hours.
 OPERART Combo Tour – PALAZZO BUTERA
Discover the Teatro Massimo and Palazzo Butera: get the combo ticket and enjoy both a guided tour of the opera house and the Valsecchi art collection in the historical Palazzo Butera. Tickets are available at the box-offices of the Teatro Massimo and Palazzo Butera. 
Operart Combo Ticket fare until June 30 2026: 17 € – Ticket fare from July 1 2026: 19 €
 OPERART Combo Tour – FONDAZIONE SICILIA
Thanks to our new partnership with the Fondazione Sicilia, a single ticket has been introduced that grants access to the Teatro Massimo, Palazzo Branciforte and Villa Zito 
for €19, or €14 for groups of at least 20 people
. This is a special opportunity to discover the architectural splendours of Basile and the Fondazione Sicilia’s exquisite art and archaeological collections.
