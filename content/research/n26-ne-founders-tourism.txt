The park features a meticulously crafted village of historic buildings open year-round, each telling the story of Nelson's commercial and social development from the 19th century onwards. Visitors can explore interactive exhibits, stroll through beautifully landscaped gardens, and discover the stories behind iconic structures. What sets Founders apart is its vibrant blend of history and community; the park hosts an array of exciting events, from the annual Book Fair to the lively Kai Festival, offering something for everyone. Whether you're looking to delve into the past, enjoy a family day out, or find the perfect venue for your special event, Founders Heritage Park provides a unique, idyllic, and immersive experience that stands out in the region.
