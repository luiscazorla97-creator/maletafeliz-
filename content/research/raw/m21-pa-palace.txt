Opening Hours Entrances and notices
It is hereby informed that, due to scheduled restoration works at the Palatine Chapel, opening and closing times will undergo temporary changes. During this period, some areas may not be accessible in order to ensure the safety of visitors and staff.
We apologize for the inconvenience and thank you for your understanding. Your support helps us preserve this unique heritage.
CLOSURE OF THE PALATINE CHAPEL
Every Sunday, the Palatine Chapel will be closed to the public from 9.30 to 11.00
EXTRAORDINARY CLOSURE NOVEMBER 2026
On Monday, November 2 and Tuesday, November 3, 2026, access to the Palatine Chapel will not be possible for visitors with disabilities, due to the temporary closure of the entrance door for restoration work on the structure.
On Tuesday, November 24, 2026, the last ticket to visit the Palatine Chapel will be issued at 4:00 PM
Please note that the Monumental Complex of the Royal Palace, as the seat of the Sicilian Parliament, and the Palatine Chapel may be subject to full or partial closures or schedule changes without prior notice due to institutional or religious reasons.

Accessibility
Accessible and Non-Accessible Areas for Visitors with Reduced Mobility
The Monumental Complex of the Royal Palace is equipped, where possible, with facilities to remove architectural barriers, ensuring maximum comfort and safety for visitors with disabilities.
Accessible Areas
Bookshop, Sale Duca di Montalto, Cappella Palatina, Sala d’Ercole, Sala dei Viceré, Sala Pompeiana, Sala Cinese e Giardini Reali.
Non-Accessible Areas
Area Archeologica delle Mura Puniche, Sala Re Ruggero e Torre Pisana.
Pet Accessibility
Access to the site is permitted only to guide dogs and dogs used for pet therapy.
Luggage Storage
The site does not have a luggage storage facility.
Animals are not allowed on the premises, even if carried in pet carriers.
