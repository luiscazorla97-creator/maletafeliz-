As of 1st April 2017, the unlimited travel ticket for foreign tourists, Sendai Area Pass, is on sale. This cheap and convenient pass allows you a day of unlimited travel on the Loople Sendai, Sendai City subway lines and buses, and JR trains in the area. You can also receive discounts on entry fees to Zuihoden and Sendai City Museum, among other locations, and other special services. For details, please see the website. Sendai Area Pass The “Sendai Marugoto Pass” allows you unlimited travel around Sendai and vicinity The pass is good for a two-day period and allows pass-holders unlimited rides on trains and buses. You can travel anywhere in Sendai as well as major sightseeing spots such as Akiu Onsen, Matsushima or Yamadera Temple. Purchase Until March 13 Adults 2,720 yen / Children 1,350 yen From March 14: Adults 2,930 yen / Children 1,470 yen Effective period of pass 2 days ●Where you can purchase the pass: “”Midori no Madoguchi”” (みどりの窓口、ticket office) at all JR stations, reserved seat ticket vending machines at JR Sendai Station, ticket vending machine at Sendai Airport Station, etc. ●How to use the pass: Go through the automatic ticket gate when using JR trains, the subway or the Sendai Airport Line. When using the Loople Sendai bus or other buses, show your pass to the driver when getting off the bus. *If you show your Marugoto Pass at the Sendai Tourist Information Center on the 2nd floor of JR Sendai Station, you will receive a coupon booklet containing discount coupons and information on special services. Sendai Marugoto Pass Tohoku Expressway pass The Tohoku Expressway Pass is a special service that allows unlimited use of Tohoku expressways through the ETC system at a flat rate. The pass user can drive on expressways at a flat rate as many times as they wish for two or more consecutive days up to fourteen days without worrying about additional charges. The service is available via rental car service companies. For more information, please visit the web site of East Nippon Expressway Company (available in English, Chinese, and Korean). East Nippon Expressway Company Subway & Train Sendai City Subway The Sendai Subway system runs through Sendai and is convenient for foreigners with announcements made in English. Especially Sendai Station, Hirosedori Station and Kotodai Koen Station in central Sendai are useful for tourists. You can ride on the subway for free using the Sendai Marugoto Pass. Sendai City Subway JR Senseki Line JR Senseki Line provides train services between Aobadori Station (Sendai City) and Ishinomaki Station (Ishinomaki City). Trains runs along the coastal area of Miyagi Prefecture. You can visit JR Sendai Station, the baseball stadium of the Tohoku Rakuten Golden Eagles (Miyaginohara Station), Mitsui Outlet Park Sendai Port (Nakanosakae Station), the Matsushima area (Matsushima Kaigan Station) located outside of Sendai City, and popular tourist sites using this line. JR Senzan Line JR Senzan Line provides train services between Sendai Station (Sendai City) and Yamagata Station (Yamagata City, Yamagata Prefecture). You can visit Sendai Toshogu Shrine (Toshogu Station) which is designated a national Important Cultural Property, Sakunami Onsen (Sakunami Station), and Yamadera (Yamadera Station) which is the popular tourist site in Yamagata using this line. JR-EAST Official Website Buses Loople Sendai The Loople Sendai bus stops at major sightseeing spots in central Sendai. If you purchase a one-day pass, it allows you unlimited rides for the day. If this is your first visit to Sendai, this is an ideal way to see the sights of the city. How to use Loople Sendai ●It takes about one hour per full circuit. ●From the JR Sendai Station West Exit Bus Pool, take the bus from Platform No.16 ●The first bus from JR Sendai Station departs at 9:00 and the last bus departs at 16:00. Buses run every 20 minutes on weekdays, and every 15 minutes on weekends, holidays, and throughout August. ●Get on the bus at the central entrance and get off at the front. When you are getting off the bus, insert your one-day pass into the ticket machine or pay the single fare. ●Announcements are made in English. ●Change can be made for ¥1,000 bills. Other types of bills cannot be changed. Sendai Airport Limousine Route Buses Buses in the Sendai area have English announcements when stopping at major sightseeing spots. There are 242 different bus routes with 48 lines that travel from central Sendai to its outskirts. The base fare is ¥150 – ¥180. There is a “¥120 zone” where you can ride the bus for only ¥120 within the area. If you use the Sendai Marugoto Pass, you can get on and off the bus anywhere for free. Fare: Adult 900yen, Child 450yen Bus stop: JR Sendai Station West Exit No.63 Sendai Seibu Liner Rental Cars Rental Cars If you bring the proper documents with you, you can drive a car in Japan (please check the laws before you leave for Japan). Almost all cars have a car navigation system installed so you can easily find your way around. Car rental companies Car rental agency counters are located at the Sendai Airport domestic terminal (1st floor). There are also car rental agency offices around JR Sendai Station. Rent The cost to rent a compact car is about ¥6500 for 24 hours. For each day thereafter, There is an additional ¥5700. How to rent a car You can rent a car directly from the agency on the day you arrive. It is recommended that you reserve a car beforehand on the Internet, or other means. But most taxi drivers speak only Japanese. Therefore, it is best to point at your destination to the driver. Fares Fares differ according to time and distance. The minimum fare for a small-sized taxi is ¥670 and if you take a taxi early in the morning or late at night, there is an extra charge.
