Ideas for having a budget-friendly travel in Nagasaki

Various passes are offered for travellers in Nagasaki, Kyushu region and Japan. These can be one of the options to make your trip convenient and budget-friendly one. To make the most, pick a pass that suits your needs and have fun exploring in Nagasaki prefecture.

Nagasaki Area Map

Nagasaki prefecture is located on the western coast of Japan's Kyushu Island.
It has a complex mainland topography, and it also has the most number of islands in all of the prefectures in Japan.

One-day / 24 hour Pass for Nagasaki City Tram
In order to take unlimited rides on trams in Nagasaki city, 2ways (mobile/paper) are given for travellers.

１．One-day/24-hour Pass (Mobile)
Through a mobile application, tickets can be quickly purchased. After your purchase is confirmed, you can freely travel around Nagasaki city by showing a screen of your smartphone when you get off. You can select the length of validity from 1day or 24hour.

*When you purchase a-day use ticket, you can only use it during the date. Whereus, for 24-hour use, you can use the ticket even crossing over the days when its within 24 hours. 

２．One-day Pass (Paper)
Paper passes can be purchased at specified locations in Nagasaki city including Nagasaki city general tourist information center.
Regarding other locations, here would be helpful. (Japanese only)  
Note; Ticket is not sold in the tram and you need to buy it before you get on.

For the first-time visitors, general information for taking trams (including how to board and exit, etc...) in Nagasaki city is on website.

 

  • One-day Pass (Mobile)
  • 24-hour Pass (Mobile)
  • One-day Pass (Paper) *in Japanese

One-Day/24-hours Pass for Nagasaki Bus, Nagasaki city

This pass can be used for one day of unlimited bus rides within the designated area of Nagasaki City and there are 2ways (mobile/paper) to purchase the pass.

１．One-day/24-hour Pass (Mobile)
You can confirm purchase proceedings via mobile application and select the length of validity from 1day or 24hour. 

２．One-day Pass (Paper)
Paper passes can be purchased at  Nagasaki Shichi Bus Terminal, Cocowalk Service Center, Nagasaki City General Tourist Information Center  (inside Nagasaki Station）and each Nagasaki Bus business office.

*When you purchase a-day use ticket, you can only use it during the date. Whereus, for 24-hour use, you can use the ticket even crossing over the days when its within 24 hours. 

  • One-day/24-hour Pass (Mobile)
  • One-day Pass (Paper), How to get on a bus

Shimabara Peninsula

  •

Shimatetsu Free Pass

"Shimatetsu Free Pass" is a very economical ticket that allows unlimited rides on Shimabara Railway trains, buses, and ferries.
This pass is available year-round and 2types of validity length (one-day use / 2-day use) are available. You can purchase this ticket at Shimabara Railway stations and bus/ferry offices.

Shimabara Megurin Ticket

The Shimabara Megurin Ticket gives you access to two of the city’s must-see attractions — Shimabara Castle and the Shimeiso Spring Garden — plus a variety of special offers including discounts at participating shops, free gift and so on.  (Valid for one day of purchase)

Northern Nagasaki Prefecture

  •

Saihi Bus One-Day Pass

The Saihi Bus One-Day Pass allows unlimited travel on regular local buses (excluding Fukuoka/Nagasaki express buses and the Ono Mame bus) for one day; these buses take you around the center of Sasebo where the Kujukushima Cruising Boat Pearl Queen and Huis Ten Bosch are located. Two types of pass can be purchased.

１．Weekday Free Pass
Available only as a digital pass (available for purchase only via a smartphone app; Japanese language only)
* The Weekday Free Pass can be used from 10:00 AM until the final bus service on the day of purchase.

２．Weekend/National Holiday Pass
Available both as a digital pass and as a paper pass. Paper passes can be purchased at the Sasebo Bus Center and at the Sasebo Tourist Information Center inside JR Sasebo Station.

  • View more *Japanese only

One-day Pass For Matsuura Railway, Sasebo/Hirado/Matsuura/Saza

One-day tickets are prepared for passengers on Matsuura Railway. Matsuura railway runs around northern area of Nagasaki prefecture (Sasebo/Saza/Hirado/Matsuura) and some area (Imari/arita) in Saga prefecture. Both mobile and paper pass are available as below.

１．One-day Pass (Mobile)
Via mobile application, you can confirm purchase proceedings quickly in advance of your trip. 

２．One-day Pass (Paper)
Paper passes can be bought at both on the train and 7 manned stations along the rail line.

  • One-day Pass For Matsuura Railway (Mobile)
  • One-day Pass For Matsuura Railway (Paper) *Japnese only

JR Kyushu Rail Pass

This pass is ideal for travelling throughout Kyushu as there are pass types available that offer access to Northern Kyushu (Fukuoka, Saga, Nagasaki, Oita and Kumamoto), Southern Kyushu (Oita, Kumamoto, Miyazaki and Kagoshima), or Kyushu in its entirety. This pass also gives users the ability to freely select seats on local trains, limited express trains, and Shinkansen (bullet trains).
Notice; When you get on the train, be sure to show your passport to prove that you are an international visitor. The passport must have visa stamp or seal for a temporary visitor.
 

  • JR Kyushu Rail Pass
  • JR Kyushu Rail Pass Online Booking


SUNQ PASS

SUNQ PASS is a free pass ticket that allows unlimited rides on almost all Expressway Bus and local buses in the seven prefectures in Kyushu and the Shimonoseki, Nagato area (Yamaguchi), as well as on some ferries. There are 5types of the ticket depending on the area and validity period.

 

  • SUNQ Pass


Nishitetsu Discount Bus Passes : Connecting Fukuoka and Nagasaki

  • Trip Kippu (Nagasaki, Sasebo and Huis Ten Bosch)

    Trip Kippu is a money-saving set of tickets which includes 3 one-way express bus tickets connecting Fukuoka with Nagasaki City, Sasebo and Huis Ten Bosch, and 1 one-way regular bus ticket connecting Sasebo with Huis Ten Bosch.

    [Where to purchase]
    ・Nishitetsu Tenjin Expressway Bus Terminal
    ・Hakata Bus Terminal
    ・Fukuoka Airport International Terminal

    View more *Japanese only

  • Fukuoka Hirado Ticket

    The Fukuoka Hirado Ticket is a set of round-trip bus tickets: it includes an express bus ticket from Fukuoka/Fukuoka Airport to Sasebo, and a local bus ticket from Sasebo to Hirado, plus tickets for the return journeys.

    [Where to purchase]
    ・Nishitetsu Tenjin Expressway Bus Terminal
    ・Hakata Bus Terminal
    ・Fukuoka Airport International Terminal

    View more *Japanese only

  • Huis Ten Bosch Ticket

    The Huis Ten Bosch Ticket is a set combining a round-trip express bus ticket from Fukuoka/Fukuoka Airport to Huis Ten Bosch/Sasebo (including the return journey), a one-way local bus ticket from Sasebo Station bus stop to Huis Ten Bosch, and a 1 DAY Passport for entry to Huis Ten Bosch.

    [Where to purchase]
    ・Nishitetsu Tenjin Expressway Bus Terminal
    ・Hakata Bus Terminal
    ・Fukuoka Airport International Terminal

    View more *Japanese only

  • Enjoy Sasebo Ticket

    The Enjoy Sasebo Ticket is a set combining a round-trip express bus ticket from Fukuoka/Fukuoka Airport to Sasebo (Huis Ten Bosch Train Line) (plus the return journey), and the Saihi Bus One-Day Pass which covers local buses in the central Sasebo area for one day (limited to weekends and national holidays only).

    [Where to purchase]
    ・Nishitetsu Tenjin Expressway Bus Terminal
    ・Hakata Bus Terminal
    ・Fukuoka Airport International Terminal

    View more *Japanese only

  • Fukuoka-Unzen Transfer Ticket

    The Fukuoka-Unzen Transfer Ticket is a money-saving travel option that combines a one-day express bus ticket from Fukuoka to the Shimabara Railway Line, and a one-way local bus ticket to the Unzen area. This flexible ticket can be used in various ways depending on your purpose of travel (sightseeing, business etc.).

    [Where to purchase]
    ・Nishitetsu Tenjin Expressway Bus Terminal
    ・Hakata Bus Terminal

    View more *Japanese only

Japan Rail Pass

The Japan Rail Pass (also known as JR Pass) is a multi-use ticket that allows you to efficiently travel throughout Japan (includng Tokyo, Osaka and Hiroshima). It is valid for journeys on all major JR national trains including Shinkansen bullet trains. Passengers can select a pass which is valid for 7, 14 or 21 days and there is the option of Standard Pass or a Green Pass (first class).

 

  • View more


Traveling between Western Japan and Northern Kyushu : Sanyo-San’in Northern Kyushu Area Pass

The Sanyo-San’in Northern Kyushu Area Pass is a money-saving pass allowing unlimited travel on trains across the Kansai area (including Kyoto, Osaka and Kobe) and the Okayama, Hiroshima, Tottori and Shimane area, including travel on the Shinkansen (bullet train) network and Limited Express trains. As it can be used for seven days, it is recommended for visitors planning to have a longer stay and travel around Western Japan.
