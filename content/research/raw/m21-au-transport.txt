Visitor's guide to public transport in Auckland
 How to pay for public transport as a visitor 
 You can pay with: 
 contactless debit or credit cards, smartphones or smart devices 
 AT HOP cards 
 paper tickets (only train and ferry services, not available on buses). 
 How often services run 
 All buses, trains and ferries mentioned on this page travel: 
 at least every 15 minutes 
 7am to 7pm 
 7 days a week. 
 No need for a timetable, just turn up and go. 
 AT Mobile app 
 To plan and track journeys by foot, bike and across our bus, train and ferry services, download the AT Mobile app. 
 Day trips around Auckland 
 Shopping
 Enjoy a range of shopping experiences and New Zealand designers. 
 Parnell village 
 Parnell’s historic buildings are filled with boutique shops and art galleries. To get there, catch the bus from 
Britomart (stop 7018)
 or near the 
Sky Tower (stop 1089)
. Ride 10 minutes then get off at the Parnell shops (stop 7188). 
 Newmarket shops 
 Next up is the enormous Westfield Newmarket shopping centre. Take the bus from the 
Parnell shops (7188)
. Ride 10 minutes then get off at stop 7203. 
 Auckland city centre 
 End the day with Commercial Bay’s luxury brands and award-winning restaurants. Take the train from 
Newmarket Station
 and ride 10 minutes to Waitematā Station. 
 Food
 Create your own full-day food tour. 
 Parnell 
 Start the day off right with breakfast in Parnell. Catch the bus from 
Britomart (7018)
 or near the 
Sky Tower (1089)
. Ride 10 minutes then get off your bus at the Parnell Shops (7188). 
 Wynyard Quarter 
 Make your way back to central Auckland for lunch in Wynyard Quarter. Take a 10-minute bus ride from the 
Parnell shops (7187)
 back to Britomart precinct (7016). Walk for 15 minutes along the Auckland waterfront and past the Viaduct Harbour to Wynyard Quarter. 
 Ponsonby Road 
 Catch the bus from 
Wynyard Quarter (1061)
 to Ponsonby Road (7215) for ice cream. This takes 9 minutes. 
 Karangahape Road 
 Get drinks and dinner on one of Auckland’s liveliest streets. Ride 5 minutes on the bus from 
Ponsonby Road (7215)
 to Karangahape Road (7131). 
 Family fun
 Head to a couple of Auckland’s most popular attractions. 
 Kelly Tarlton’s 
 New Zealand’s most iconic aquarium is just 12 minutes from Britomart. Bus from 
Commerce Street (7019)
 to Kelly Tarlton’s (7320). 
 Auckland city centre 
 Bus 12 minutes back to Commerce Street (7019) from 
Kelly Tarlton’s (7323)
. 
 Auckland War Memorial Museum 
 A world-class museum in a hundred-year-old building. It overlooks the city from the Auckland Domain. Ride 6 minutes on the bus from 
Britomart precinct (7018)
 to Ayr Street (7196). Walk 8 minutes along Domain Drive to Auckland War Memorial Museum. 
 Wynyard Quarter and Devonport
 Dine outdoors then take a short ferry ride to the quaint seaside village of Devonport. 
 Wynyard Quarter 
 Head to Wynyard Quarter, a waterfront precinct known for its relaxed vibe, scenic location and modern New Zealand cuisine. From the Downtown Ferry Terminal, walk 15 minutes along the Auckland Waterfront. Keep going past the Viaduct Harbour to Wynyard Quarter. 
 Devonport 
 Enjoy the heritage and coastal beauty of Devonport, accessible by a relaxing and affordable ferry ride. Walk back to the 
Downtown Ferry Terminal (15 minutes)
. Then take the 12-minute ferry ride to Devonport. 
 Harbour Bridge views and Takapuna
 A short bus ride over the harbour bridge to the beach suburb of Takapuna for shopping, water activities and outdoor dining. 
 Takapuna 
 Take the short scenic bus ride with views of the sparkling Waitematā Harbour and city skyline to Takapuna. This is a vibrant beachside suburb with a stunning white sand beach and lively hospitality scene. Catch the 82 bus from 
SkyCity Auckland stop (1332)
. Ride for 17 minutes across the Harbour Bridge to Takapuna (3568). 
 Back to the city centre 
 Take the bus back from 
Takapuna (3551)
. Ride 17 minutes to Wellesley Street (7041). 
 History
 See world-class exhibitions in Auckland’s architecturally-renowned museums. 
 Auckland War Memorial Museum 
 Head to the Auckland War Memorial Museum for world-class exhibitions. Get on the bus at 
Britomart (7018)
 and ride 6 minutes to Ayr Street (7196). Walk 8 minutes along Domain Drive to Auckland War Memorial Museum. 
 Auckland Art Gallery 
 New Zealand’s largest and most inspiring art gallery, featuring over 17,000 artworks. Walk 8 minutes from the Auckland War Memorial Museum to 
Ayr Street (7195)
. Ride 11 minutes on the bus toward the city centre and get off near Auckland University of Technology (AUT) (7089). From there, it is a 3-minute walk to the Auckland Art Gallery. 
 Volcanoes
 An easy climb up Mount Eden / Maungawhau, the highest volcano in Auckland. You will get spectacular views of the harbour and city. 
 Mount Eden shops 
 Take a 15-minute bus ride from 
Commerce Street (7026)
 to Tahaki Reserve stop (1870). 
 Mount Eden summit 
 Walk to Hillside Cresent North and up Puhi Huia Road then follow the track up Mount Eden. The walk is 2.1km long. It takes 40 minutes and you climb 3,510 steps. 
 Wildlife
 Experience Auckland's incredible wildlife, from whale and dolphin encounters in the Hauraki Gulf to spotting a kiwi at the Auckland Zoo. 
 Whale and dolphin spotting 
 Lucky passengers on Auckland’s ferries may spot dolphins in the wild. From the Downtown Ferry Terminal, take the ferry to: 
 Devonport (12 minutes) 
 Waiheke Island (40 minutes) 
 Half Moon Bay (40 minutes)
. 
 Otherwise, walk 8 minutes to the Viaduct Harbour to find dolphin and whale safari tours. 
 Auckland Zoo 
 Get on the bus at 
Stop D Lower Albert (1005)
 and ride for 15 minutes to Auckland Zoo (stop 8124). 
 Exciting adventures
 A full day of thrills, from water sports to conquering the heights of the Harbour Bridge with a climb or bungy jump. 
 Z Pier water sports 
 Bus to Z Pier for water sports like kayaking, jetboating and jet skiing. To get there, go to 
Stop A Lower Albert (1002)
 or 
Stop E Lower Albert (1006)
. Ride 4 minutes to stop 7036. 
 Z Pier is 7 minutes’ walk from stop 7036. 
 Auckland Harbour Bridge 
 Climb the Harbour Bridge or bungy jump off the top of it. The Harbour Bridge is a 20-minute walk from Z Pier.
