Access to the nearby areas

From Kurashiki Station to Kojima: Approx.16 km
  • Approximately a 1-hour ride on a local bus to JR Kojima Station
  • Approximately a 17-minute train ride to Okayama Station on the JR Sanyo Main Line.
    Approximately a 25-minute train ride to Kojima Station from Okayama Station on the JR Seto-Ohashi Line
From Kurashiki Station to Mabi: Approx.10 km
  • Approximately a 7-minute train ride to Kiyone Station on the JR Hakubi Line.
    Approximately a 7-minute train ride to Kibinomakibi Station from Kiyone Station on the Ibara Line
From Kurashiki Station to Tamashima: Approx.9 km
  • Approximately an 8-mintute train ride on the JR Sanyo Main Line to Shin-Kurashiki
    Station and then approximately 20-minute drive to Tamashima
From Kurashiki Station to Chayamachi: Approx.6 km
  • Approximately 25-minute ride on a local bus to Chayamachi Station
