FAQ

Where does the yacht depart from?

We are departing from Dubai Marina

Do you offer transfers?

We do not offer transfers, please make your own way: Xclusive Marina – Behind Al Majara Towers
  • By Metro: arrive at DMCC Metro Station, walk toward Nuran Residences
  • By Taxi: drop off at Al Majara Towers
  • By Car: parking at Marina Mall (3 hrs free)

Where is the boarding point?

Meet & Greet point is at Xclusive Marina-Behind Al Majara Tower

Do you have parking facilities?

By Car: we recommend you to park at Marina Mall (3 hrs free)

Can I bring my own food and alcohol?

We recommend you to add food through your booking

Can we go for a swim?

No. Swimming is strictly prohibited.

Are babies allowed onboard?

Yes. Kids under 2 years are permitted but must remain within the Saloon or Cabin area accompanied by an adult. Children under the age of 9 are required to wear life jackets throughout the charter.

What if I am late?

As this is a private charter you can come anytime from 8:30pm onwards, however all boats must return by 2am to dock.

Is there fireworks display?

At the stroke of midnight, fireworks show is expected to occur at JBR, Burj Al Arab and Atlantis the Palm.

What do I need to bring?

As per UAE law, all guests must bring a copy of a valid ID. Please make sure you have a copy on your phone: a passport copy for tourists; Emirates ID, passport or UAE driving licence for UAE residents. Non compliance may result in a fine of up to AED 1,500 per person and unnecessary disruption to your experience.
