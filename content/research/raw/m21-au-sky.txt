 How do I get to SkyCity Auckland? 
 keyboard_arrow_down 
Te Waihorotiu Station (Albert Street) is on the door-step of SkyCity, and there are also several bus routes stopping nearby along Albert, Wellesley and Queen Streets. By car, it's easily reached from Auckland's motorway network - from the Southern Motorway, exit at Nelson Street and follow the signs to SkyCity. Auckland Airport is around 20–30 minutes away.

About Sky Tower
Standing 328 metres above Auckland, the Sky Tower has dominated the Tāmaki Makaurau skyline for over 29 years.
From 186 metres above street level, enjoy panoramic 360° views stretching up to 80 kilometres in every direction — spot the city's 53 volcanoes, the Hauraki Gulf islands, and landmarks across Tāmaki Makaurau.
Admission Options
Sky Tower Admission
Access Main Observation Deck (186m), The Lookout (194m), and *SkyBar (182m).
*SkyBar is an R18+ licensed venue open from 11am daily. Valid photo ID may be required
Sky Tower Admission plus Sky Deck
Access all of the above, plus the Sky Deck (220m) — New Zealand's highest viewing deck.
Tickets are valid for six months from purchase. All children must be accompanied by an adult. SkyBar is R18+ only – valid photo ID required.
Join the Sky Tower whānau and experience the best views all year round. Pass holders enjoy 15% off the Sky Tower Gift Shop (excluding alcohol, max $50), 20% off SkyWalk, SkyRide, SkyBar, All Blacks Experience, and Wētā Workshop Unleashed, and 25% off Magic Memories souvenir photos.

Frequently Asked Questions
 Can I request the Sky Tower to be lit for my cause? 
 keyboard_arrow_down 
Every year the Sky Tower receives hundreds of requests to light up Auckland to support charities, mark national occasions and express solidarity with communities.
We are currently fulfilling established charity and partner commitments and will only reply if your request is successful due to the high demand we are experiencing.
To submit a request fill out this form: 
Request Sky Tower lighting
 Can I hire the Sky Tower for a private event? 
 keyboard_arrow_down 
Yes — New Zealand's most iconic landmark is available for private events 220 metres above Auckland. The Sky Tower has three hireable spaces: the Main Observation Area at 186 metres (Level 51), The Lookout at 192 metres (Level 53), and the Sky Deck at 220 metres (Level 60). Whether you're planning a sunrise corporate breakfast, cocktail reception, or evening celebration, our events team can help bring your vision to life above the Auckland skyline. To enquire, email 
events@skycity.co.nz
 or call +64 9 363 6000.

 Can I use my SHOW by SkyCity Rewards for parking? 
 keyboard_arrow_down 
Yes, if you have sufficient SHOW by SkyCity Points or a car parking credit accrued on your card. Your SHOW by SkyCity Rewards card can be swiped at the parking machines to exit. Please note, some car parking credits are required to be activated at a SHOW by SkyCity Rewards Kiosk before redemption.
 What is the height restriction for the car parks? 
 keyboard_arrow_down 
The maximum height for vehicles in SkyCity Main Site car park is 2.1 metres and in SkyCity Grand Hotel car park is 2.1 metres.
